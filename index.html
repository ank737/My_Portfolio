About

About me

I’m a third-year B.Tech IT student interested in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and building technology that solves practical problems. I’m more curious about the “why” behind a problem than simply the technology used to solve it—understanding what makes a process difficult, where time or resources are being lost, and whether there’s a simpler or smarter way to approach it.

I learn by building, experimenting, and turning ideas into working solutions, taking them from development to deployment. Through my projects, I’ve explored AI/ML, data analysis, backend systems, APIs, and web technologies while learning how different pieces come together to create something useful.

I enjoy discovering problems worth solving, learning from people with different perspectives and experience, and exploring what becomes possible when good ideas and technology come together. For me, the most interesting part of technology isn’t just what we can build, but what becomes possible when we build it for the right reason.
